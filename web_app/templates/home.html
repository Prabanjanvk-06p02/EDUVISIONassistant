How EduVision AI Works

High-throughput pipeline orchestrated across client and server

1
Hand Landmark Extraction
MediaPipe extracts 21 3D coordinates per hand at full camera FPS in an isolated thread.
2
Compiled Neural Inference
A JIT-compiled TensorFlow graph evaluates normalized coordinates in ~3ms without Python GIL overhead.
3
Neural Language Intelligence
Raw sign keywords are transformed into fluent, grammatically natural sentences with ADVANCED NEURAL AI.
4
Speech-to-Text & TTS
Instant offline vocalization via pyttsx3 paired with client Web Speech recognition and summaries.
